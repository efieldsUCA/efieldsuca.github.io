Who I am

I'm an Engineering Physics graduate from the University of Central Arkansas (ABET-accredited, May 2026) and a first-generation college student, and a member of IEEE Region 5. My work sits at the intersection of physics and hardware. I'm most at home designing, building, and debugging real systems rather than just modeling them on paper.

I put myself through school working full time, which shaped how I approach engineering: practical, resourceful, and comfortable owning a problem end to end. Right now I'm doing R&D engineering work focused on experimental design and dimensional-stability analysis of manufactured parts.

Outside of that, I build things for the fun of it! I build Autonomous robots, indulge in automotive projects, and play a variety of instruments. Most of what I know I learned by taking something apart and putting it back together.
